Dear Mr. Gao, dear Guest Editors Prof. Jong-min Kim and Prof. Rob Kim Marjerison,

We submit the second revision of manuscript systems-4669426, "Is the Optimiser Needed? Auditing a Digital Decision-Support System for Necessity, Decision Stability and Input Fragility under Turbulence". We thank Reviewer 3 for confirming that the first revision addressed their remarks, and Reviewer 2 for a second careful report. A point-by-point response to Reviewer 2 is attached, and all changes are marked in the version with changes highlighted.

The main changes are:

- **Corrected objective.** As Reviewer 2 asked, the input-fragility study (RQ3) and the repair grid (RQ4) are repeated under the corrected prioritisation objective, in a new Section 5.5. The repair results hold. The ranking of the inputs changes: the person-side inputs lose most of their weight. The paper now reports this and draws the consequence for the recommendations.
- **Deployment envelope.** Its conclusions now rest on the hard-capacity results, which are operationally feasible. The time limits are described as solver-time limits, with total latency reported separately.
- **Clarifications.** A diagnosis of a non-monotone result of the seeded evolutionary search, a clearer scope statement for the two further models, and editorial corrections, including the appendix numbering.
- **Supplementary Materials.** The full fuzzy rule bases, the per-instance envelope table and the 36-condition input-fragility study under the corrected objective are now provided in a supplementary file.

**Status of the related correction.** Reviewer 2 asked us to inform you of the status of the correction to our earlier article (Applied Sciences 2026, 16, 7581, doi:10.3390/app16157581). On 8 November 2026 all three authors submitted a correction request to Applied Sciences, covering the prioritisation objective and the description of the CAIL objective. It is under editorial review, and Section 6.5 of the manuscript describes it. We will inform you as soon as it is decided, so that the published version of this article can cite it.

The code, instances and result manifests of this revision, including the reruns under the corrected objective, are archived at Zenodo as version 0.6.1 (https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24250168).

All authors have approved the revised version.
