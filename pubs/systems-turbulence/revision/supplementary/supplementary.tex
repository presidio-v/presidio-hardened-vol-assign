\section*{S1. Rule bases of the audited fuzzy systems}
Tables~S1--S9 give the complete rule bases of the five fuzzy inference systems of the audited
system, as published in~[10] of the article. They are generated from the rule constants of
the open implementation (module \texttt{presidio\_vol\_assign.allocation.fis}, script
\texttt{experiments/make\_supplementary\_rules.py}), so they are the rules every result of the
article was computed with. Inputs, terms, membership breakpoints, inference and
defuzzification are given in Table~3 of the article. Output terms: VL very low, L low, M
moderate, H high, VH very high. FIS$_1$ and FIS$_3$ have three inputs and are shown as one
grid per level of their first input. The three-objective model of the article uses FIS$_1$,
FIS$_2$ and FIS$_3$; FIS$_{2a}$ and FIS$_{2b}$ belong to the four-objective model of~[10].
