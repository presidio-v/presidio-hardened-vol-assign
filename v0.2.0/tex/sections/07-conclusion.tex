\section*{Code and Data Availability}
The implementation (presidio-hardened-vol-assign \texttt{v0.2.0}) is openly
available at \url{https://github.com/presidio-v/presidio-hardened-vol-assign}
(MIT licence; archived release with a DOI on Zenodo). Every figure and table in
this paper is reproduced deterministically from a fixed seed: the synthetic
datasets live under \texttt{examples/} (regenerated by
\texttt{examples/generate\_examples.py}), \texttt{pva benchmark} regenerates the
metric tables, and \texttt{pva show} regenerates the figures.
