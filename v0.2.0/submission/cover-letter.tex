\opening{Dear Guest Editors,}

We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled \textbf{``From Methodology to
Practice: A Production-Grade Decision Support Tool for Multi-Objective
Humanitarian Allocation in Resilient Supply Chains''} for consideration in
the Special Issue ``Innovations in Supply Chain Resilience.''

Our contribution sits squarely at the intersection of three SI themes:
multi-objective optimisation as a \emph{risk-mitigation framework}, hardened
open-source tooling as a \emph{digital-technology innovation}, and the
explicit treatment of post-disaster humanitarian flows as a stress-case of
\emph{supply chain resilience}.

Specifically, we make three contributions:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item A methodological framework combining three Mamdani Fuzzy Inference
        Systems (Fairness Level in People Prioritization, Transportation
        Feasibility Level, Center Allocation Balance Level) with NSGA-II and
        NRGA evolutionary optimisation, producing Pareto-optimal allocations
        of affected people to relief centres.
  \item A production-grade, security-hardened, reproducible Python
        implementation released as \texttt{presidio-hardened-vol-assign}
        v0.2.0 (open-source), positioning operational tooling itself as a
        digital-innovation contribution to SCR practice.
  \item An extended evaluation incorporating reproducibility metrics
        alongside the established convergence / diversity / hypervolume
        analyses.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Disclosure of related work.} A methodology-only precursor of this
work is currently under peer review at \emph{Autonomous Transportation
Research} (Elsevier), manuscript number ATRES-S-26-00021. The present
submission is substantially distinct: it adds the open-source production
implementation (Section~4), the supply-chain-resilience framing throughout,
and the reproducibility-focused evaluation (Section~5.4). Together these
represent a majority of the manuscript's content and constitute the
contribution targeted at the Applied Sciences readership.

The work has not been published previously in any form and is not under
consideration for publication elsewhere apart from the disclosure above.
All authors have approved the submission.

We thank the Guest Editors and reviewers for considering our work.
